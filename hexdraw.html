Tools act on the active layer; a new layer starts one spacing above the last. The chip sets the layer's ink — its bonds, labels and arrows.

bond — new atoms bond to their lattice neighbours. free — off the honeycomb: rings are regular n-gons placed where you press, nothing snaps or auto-bonds, and hydrogens follow the bond orders you draw. Templates need a free layer and tick it for you.
